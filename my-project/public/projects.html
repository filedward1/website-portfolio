NutriXtract

University of the East — Manila | Thesis Project

August 2024 – May 2025

Enhancing nutritional data literacy through an AI-driven pipeline for object detection and label interpretation. Led development of a React Native, FastAPI, and Supabase mobile app that uses OCR and YOLO-based object detection to interpret nutritional data, increasing user nutritional awareness from 31% to 92%. Engineered an explainable AI pipeline using large language models to convert raw data into personalized dietary insights, achieving an F1-score of 0.8612 and a 2.19% character error rate validated by medical professionals. Published and presented the technical paper at AAIML 2026 at Chuo University in Tokyo, Japan (IEEE SMCS) and earned selection for Global Presentation at HCI International 2025 in Gothenburg, Sweden. Qualified for AppCon 2024 as one of the Top 90 teams out of 178 applicants nationwide.
